Slide 3 / 10 • Audit Finding #1

Finding #1: The Currency Cliff & Departmental Disconnect

The 2015 "Currency Cliff"

45% of books were published before 2015, averaging <2.1 checkouts (67 had 0 checkouts). In contrast, 2021–2024 syllabus editions circulate 35–62 times/year. Students read—when the library has modern syllabus books.

Departmental Disparity

Management & CS account for 68% of total campus engagement. Humanities and Basic Sciences remain detached due to 100% obsolete physical collections and zero syllabus-aligned e-resources.

Slide 4 / 10 • Audit Finding #2

Finding #2: Financial Leakage in High-Cost Journals

₹11.00 Lakh Drain

60 specialized journal subscriptions consume over 61% of total library funds.

60.6% Dormancy

40 out of 66 sampled journals recorded fewer than 10 downloads all year.

₹6,500+ / Download

Niche print periodicals yield abysmal ROI compared to digital e-resource bundles.

Decision: Dropping the 40 dormant journal subscriptions saves ₹7.20 Lakh annually with zero negative impact on undergraduate or postgraduate coursework.

Slide 5 / 10 • Audit Finding #3

Finding #3: The 5:00 PM Timetable Lockout

Hourly Footfall Distribution

• 09:00 - 11:00 AM: 32.0% (Morning gap)

• 11:00 - 01:00 PM: 38.3% (Lunch peak)

• 01:00 - 03:00 PM: 19.7%

• 03:00 - 05:00 PM: 10.0% (60% footfall collapse)

Root Cause: Timetable Clashes

82% of student laboratory sessions and afternoon seminars run until 4:30 PM. Shutting doors at 5:00 PM locks out day scholars right when their academic day finishes. Extending to 8:30 PM is mandatory.

Slide 7 / 10 • Action Plan

The 3 High-Impact Turnaround Actions

1. Night Owl Hours

Extend weekday opening to 08:30 AM – 08:30 PM using staggered staff & PG student assistants. Unlocks 3.5 hrs after classes.

2. Course Reserve Bank

Dedicate ₹4.80L to stock 5 reserve copies of all mandatory 2024 course textbooks with guaranteed short-term/overnight borrowing.

3. Collaborative Commons

Weed 14,000 dead pre-2015 volumes to create a Co-working Zone with mobile whiteboards, Wi-Fi, and laptop power sockets.
