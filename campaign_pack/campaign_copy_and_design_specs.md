# RIMS Library Campaign Pack: Creative Brief & Canva AI Production Guide

**Campaign Theme:** "Your Campus Living Room: More Books, Better Hours, Zero Excuses"  
**Target Audience:** 3,500 Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students across Management, Computer Science, Commerce, Humanities, and Basic Sciences.  
**Deliverables:**
1. **1 × Instagram Post (1080 × 1080 px)**
2. **1 × Campus Noticeboard Poster (A3 Portrait: 297 × 420 mm / 3508 × 4960 px)**

## Deliverable 4A: Instagram Square Post (1080 × 1080 px)

### Strategic Message & Data Point
- **Core Message:** "We Heard You. The Library Just Got an Upgrade."
- **Data Point from Analysis:** *"60% of students couldn't make it before 5:00 PM due to classes. So we unlocked night hours until 8:30 PM!"*
- **Call-to-Action (CTA):** *"Tag your study buddy & claim your desk tonight! Swipe up / visit link in bio to check out the new 2024 Reserve Textbook catalog."*

### Canva AI Text Prompt (Copy-Pasteable into Canva "Magic Media / Text to Image"):
> `Modern, energetic university library at twilight, glowing warm study lamps, sleek minimalist wooden desks, college students collaborating with laptops and highlighters, large floor-to-ceiling glass windows showing evening sky, vibrant navy blue and neon amber lighting accents, high resolution, photorealistic, premium editorial academic atmosphere --ar 1:1`

### Copy Layout Structure
- **Header Badge:** `NEW AT RIMS CENTRAL LIBRARY`
- **Hook (Bold 72pt):** `NO MORE 5 PM CURFEW.`
- **Data Stat Callout Box:**  
  `[ 60% of students were locked out by class schedules. ]`  
  `➔ WE ARE NOW OPEN UNTIL 8:30 PM.`
- **Feature Icons / Bullets:**
  - ⚡ 100+ Brand-New 2024 Syllabus Editions
  - 💻 High-Speed Wi-Fi & Laptop Charging at Every Desk
  - ☕ Collaborative Group Study Commons
- **Footer CTA:** `Your evenings belong here. Mon-Fri | 8:30 AM - 8:30 PM`

### Strategic Message & Data Point
- **Core Message:** "Top Grades Aren't Built on 10-Year-Old PDFs. Modern Books for Modern Minds."
- **Data Point from Analysis:** *"Pre-2015 books averaged fewer than 2 checkouts. We weeded the obsolete stacks and stocked 2024 syllabus editions for every single major!"*
- **Call-to-Action (CTA):** *"Scan the QR code to reserve your semester textbooks today or visit the 1st Floor Reserve Desk before stock runs out!"*

### Canva AI Text Prompt:
> `Bold modern typography poster for a prestigious university library, navy blue and vivid warm gold color scheme, clean layout with geometric accents, visual of sleek open modern textbooks with futuristic holographic data charts floating above, energetic and academic, Swiss graphic design style, high contrast, clean negative space, A3 format --ar 1:1.41`

- **Headline (Display Font):**

  `SO WERE WE.`
